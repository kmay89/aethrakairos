Privacy

Aethra Kairos — the website, the Mac app and the Apple TV app. Effective November 6, 2026.

The short version

Aethra Kairos does not collect personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no cookies and no third-party SDKs, and nothing about you is sold or shared. Like every website and app that streams, your device has to ask servers for files, and those servers see the request. This page says exactly which servers, and what they see.

Who we are

Aethra Kairos is made by ERRERlabs ("we" on this page). For anything about this policy or your data, use the support page. If you would rather not ask in public, say only that you have a private request, and we will give you a private address to write to.

What stays on your device

To remember things for you, the apps keep data on your own device only. It is never sent to us.

  • Your library: the songs you heart, what you played recently, where you left off, your queue, your saved journeys and playlists.
  • Your settings: for example Reduce Flashing (CALM on the web), Auto Rooms, the look, the lens, and whether you have seen the welcome or a tip.
  • Caches: the songs you play, the music catalog and the player itself are cached so they play smoothly, work offline and are not fetched twice. The player also keeps its own analysis of songs (for example where two songs mix well).

In a browser and the Mac app this lives in the site's storage (local storage, IndexedDB and the offline cache). On Apple TV it lives in the app's own storage. Clearing the site's data in your browser, or deleting the app, removes all of it.

What servers see

When your device asks a server for a file, the server receives standard request information: your IP address, the time, the file asked for and your browser or app version. The servers involved are:

  • aethrakairos.com, hosted by Netlify — the website, the player and the music catalog.
  • media.aethrakairos.com, Cloudflare R2 — the songs.
  • github.com — the Mac app checks for updates here when it starts and every 12 hours.

These providers process that information to deliver the files and keep the service secure, and keep it for their own limited log periods. We do not use it to identify you, build a profile or follow you anywhere, and we do not combine it with anything. The website loads no fonts, scripts or images from anyone else: everything it needs is served from aethrakairos.com.

If you open the player with a link to someone else's catalog, your device fetches that catalog and its songs from their server, under their own policy.

Optional features

  • Microphone (website and Mac app). The mic-reactive visual and the piano lessons listen through your microphone, only after you switch them on and allow it. The sound is analysed on your device, moment to moment, to move the picture or hear a note. It is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. The Apple TV app does not use a microphone.
  • Stage (website). To pair screens with a booth, the devices find each other through a small mailbox service at kmay89.com, run by the same maker. It passes a four-letter room code, a secret key that lets the booth reclaim its room, the booth's current colour and scene, and the connection details browsers exchange to connect directly. Those connection details include the devices' IP addresses. A room is forgotten after about a quarter of an hour of quiet. To find a route through your router, browsers also ask a public STUN server run by Google (stun.l.google.com), which sees your IP address. The picture then travels directly between your devices, so each device in a stage can see the others' IP addresses.
  • Smart lights (Mac app). If you connect Philips Hue lights, the app talks to your Hue bridge on your own local network. Nothing leaves your home for this.
  • Sharing and clips. When you share a link or save a clip, it goes where you send it, through your device's own share sheet or downloads. We are not part of that.

Apple TV

The Apple TV app has no account and no sign-in, uses no advertising identifier, and contains no analytics or tracking code. It talks only to aethrakairos.com and media.aethrakairos.com. Its App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected. Apple runs the App Store under its own privacy policy; if you have chosen in your device's settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may show us anonymous, aggregated statistics such as crash counts.

Legal basis and transfers

Where the law (for example the GDPR or UK GDPR) asks for a legal basis, the request information above is handled on the basis of legitimate interests: delivering what your device asked for and keeping the service secure. Our providers are based in the United States and may process it there or elsewhere; they rely on recognised safeguards such as the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to your data protection authority. We hold no accounts or profiles, so in practice there is usually nothing to find, but ask anyway and we will answer, and pass on any request that concerns our providers' logs. Everything kept on your device is yours to delete at any time.

California and other U.S. states: we do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals are honoured by default, because there is nothing to opt out of.

Children

Aethra Kairos is made for a general audience, children included. Because it collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with its new date. Earlier versions are kept in the project's public history on GitHub.
