Privacy

Aethra Kairos — the website, the Mac app and the Apple TV app. Last updated November 6, 2026.

The short version

Aethra Kairos does not collect personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs, and nothing you do is sold or shared.

What stays on your device

To remember things for you, the apps keep a little data on your own device only: the songs you heart, what you played recently, where you left off, and your settings (for example Reduce Flashing or Auto Rooms). In a browser this lives in the site's local storage; on Apple TV and Mac it lives in the app's own storage. It is never sent to us. Deleting the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, removes it.

Music you play is downloaded and cached on the device so it can play smoothly and does not have to be fetched twice.

What a server sees

To play anything, your device has to ask for it. The website and the music catalog are served from aethrakairos.com (hosted by Netlify) and the songs from media.aethrakairos.com (Cloudflare R2). Like every web server, these providers handle standard request information — your IP address, the time, the file asked for and your browser or app version — to deliver the files and to keep the service secure. We do not use it to identify you, build a profile, or follow you anywhere, and we do not combine it with anything.

Optional features

  • Stage (website). When you pair screens to a booth, a four-letter room code and the connection details your browsers need to find each other pass through a small mailbox service at kmay89.com. The picture then travels directly between your devices. Nothing about you is stored.
  • Smart lights (Mac app). If you connect Philips Hue lights, the app talks to your Hue bridge on your own local network. Nothing leaves your home for this.

Apple TV

The Apple TV app has no account and no sign-in, uses no advertising identifier, and contains no analytics or tracking code. Its App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected.

Children

Aethra Kairos is made for everyone, and because it collects no personal data, it collects none from children either.

Questions

Ask anything on the support page. If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with its date.
