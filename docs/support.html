Apple TV — the remote

Start the music
On the welcome screen pick a mood — Party, Chill or Shuffle. It plays nonstop, blended from song to song.
Play / pause
Click the touch surface, or press ▶︎❚❚.
Next / previous song
Swipe or press right / left.
Change the picture
Swipe or press up / down to move between light shows.
Browse
Press Menu (or <) to bring up albums, moods and settings; press it again to go to the Home Screen.
Love a song
Press and hold the touch surface.

Common questions

The music won't start
The songs stream over the internet. Check that the Apple TV (or your computer) is online; the app keeps trying and starts by itself once the connection is back.
The light shows flash too much
Turn on Reduce Flashing in Settings.
It's free?
Yes — no account, no ads, no subscription.
What do you collect?
Nothing personal. See the privacy page.
